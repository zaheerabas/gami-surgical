Cuticle pushers are used in almost every manicure and pedicure to push back the eponychium and lift dead cuticle tissue from the nail plate. A good pusher has smooth, well-shaped ends that work gently without scratching the nail. Most professional cuticle pushers are double-ended, combining two shapes in one tool.

In short

Choose a rounded or square pusher end that matches the nail shape, and a second end (scraper, hatchet or narrow pusher) for cleaning the nail plate and sidewalls. Edges must be smooth enough not to scratch the nail.

Common cuticle pusher ends

EndUse
Rounded (spoon) pusherPushing back cuticles on rounded nails
Square or flat pusherSquare nail shapes and wider nails
Narrow pusherSmall nails and corners
Scraper (blade-like edge)Removing dead tissue from the nail plate
Hatchet / triangularCleaning sidewalls and the nail fold
Nail cleaner (pointed)Cleaning under the free edge

How to use a cuticle pusher

Soften the cuticle first with water or a cuticle remover. Hold the pusher at a low angle to the nail and use light pressure to push the cuticle back and lift dead tissue. Pressing too hard or holding the pusher too steeply can damage the nail plate and the nail matrix.

Stainless steel quality

Professional cuticle pushers are made of stainless steel so they can be cleaned and disinfected between clients. The working ends should be polished and their edges finished so they are effective but not sharp enough to cut skin.

Before you buy

  • Ends are smooth and evenly finished, with no burrs.
  • Scraper edges are effective but not knife-sharp.
  • The handle is textured for a secure grip.
  • The tool is comfortable and balanced in the hand.

Frequently asked questions

Should cuticle pushers be sharp?

No. Pusher ends should be smooth. Scraper ends can have a fine edge, but nothing that cuts the skin.

Metal or wooden pushers?

Wooden sticks are single-use. Stainless steel pushers are reusable after cleaning and disinfection and last for years.

Which shape should a salon stock?

A double-ended pusher with a rounded pusher and a scraper or hatchet end suits most manicures.
