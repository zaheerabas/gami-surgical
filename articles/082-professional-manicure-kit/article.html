Whether you are a new nail technician or equipping a salon, a professional manicure kit should contain reliable tools that can be cleaned and disinfected between clients. This guide lists the essentials and explains how to plan enough sets.

In short

A professional manicure kit contains cuticle nippers, a cuticle pusher, cuticle scissors, nail clippers or nail scissors, and files and buffers. Metal tools are reusable after disinfection; files and buffers are usually single-use.

Reusable metal tools

ToolPurpose
Cuticle nippers (half or quarter jaw)Trimming dead cuticle and hangnails
Cuticle pusher (double-ended)Pushing back cuticles, cleaning the nail plate
Cuticle scissorsFine trimming of hangnails
Nail clippers or nail scissorsShortening nails
Nail cleanerCleaning under the free edge
Stainless steel fileShaping (reusable if disinfectable)

Single-use items

Emery boards, buffers, orangewood sticks and similar porous items cannot be disinfected reliably. In most places they must be used once and then thrown away or given to the client.

How many sets?

Each client needs clean, disinfected tools. Disinfection takes time, so plan at least two or three sets per technician, more for busy salons. Count appointments per day and the time your disinfection routine takes.

Kit checklist

  • Stainless steel tools that can be cleaned and disinfected.
  • Enough sets for back-to-back appointments.
  • Separate containers for used and clean tools.
  • A supply of single-use files and buffers.
  • Caps or a case to protect tips during storage.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most important tool in a manicure kit?

Most technicians say good cuticle nippers, because poor nippers tear skin and slow down every appointment.

Can metal nail files be reused?

Stainless steel files can be reused if they can be cleaned and disinfected according to local rules.

Can manicure kits be made for a brand?

Yes. Manufacturers can supply kits in custom cases with branded tools; ask about options.
