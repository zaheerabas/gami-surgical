Thinning scissors remove bulk from hair and soften lines without changing the overall length. One blade (or both) has teeth that cut only some of the hair in each closure. The number and shape of the teeth decide how much hair is removed and the effect it gives, so it pays to choose thinning scissors carefully.

In short

More, finer teeth remove less hair per cut and give a soft blend. Fewer, wider teeth remove more hair and create texture (chunking). Choose by the effect you want, not the tooth count alone.

Teeth and removal rate

Thinning scissors are often described by their number of teeth, commonly from about 6 to 40, and by their approximate removal rate, the percentage of hair cut in a single closure. The tooth spacing and groove shape also matter.

TypeTeethEffect
Blending / finishingMany fine teeth (for example 30 to 40)Soft blending, removes lines, low removal
ThinningMedium count (for example 20 to 30)Removes bulk evenly
Texturising / chunkingFewer, wider teeth (for example 6 to 15)Visible texture, high removal

Single- or double-sided teeth

Most thinning scissors have teeth on one blade and a straight edge on the other. Double-sided versions have teeth on both blades and remove hair more gently.

Notched or V-shaped teeth

Teeth may have small notches or grooves that hold the hair as the blades close, preventing it from sliding and giving a more consistent cut. Smooth teeth tend to give a softer result.

Before you buy

  • The teeth are evenly spaced and polished, so they do not pull hair.
  • The blades close smoothly with correct tension.
  • The handle design matches your cutting scissors for comfort.
  • The length suits your techniques (often 5.5 to 6.5 inches).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between thinning and texturising scissors?

Thinning scissors remove bulk evenly with many fine teeth. Texturising (chunking) scissors have fewer, wider teeth and remove larger sections for visible texture.

How many teeth should my first thinning scissors have?

Many stylists start with around 25 to 30 teeth, which gives a versatile, even result.

Can thinning scissors be sharpened?

Yes, by a specialist who sharpens hairdressing scissors.
