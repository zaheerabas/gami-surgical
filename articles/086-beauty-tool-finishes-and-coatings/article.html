The finish of a beauty tool affects how it looks on a shelf, how it feels in the hand and how well it stands up to cleaning. For brands and distributors, beauty tool finishes are also part of the product identity. This guide explains the common options.

In short

Satin finishes hide fine scratches; mirror finishes look bright and are easy to wipe clean. Coloured coatings help branding but must be durable, and should stay off the precise working tips.

Coatings and colours

  • Powder or paint coatings give bright colours and patterns, popular for tweezers and retail kits.
  • Titanium and PVD coatings give metallic colours such as gold, rose gold, black or rainbow, with good hardness.
  • Soft-touch or rubberised grips improve comfort on handles.

Keep coatings off the working tips

On tweezers, nippers and scissors, the working surfaces must stay bare steel or be specially finished. A coating on tweezer tips or cutting edges can chip, reduce grip or dull the cut. Good manufacturers mask these areas.

Durability and cleaning

Questions to ask a manufacturer

  • How does the coating stand up to the disinfectants used in salons?
  • Is the coating applied over properly prepared, passivated steel?
  • Are working tips and edges left uncoated?
  • Can samples be tested for chipping and fading?

Frequently asked questions

Do coloured coatings wear off?

Some cheaper coatings do. Ask for samples and test them with your cleaning routine before ordering in volume.

Is a mirror finish better than satin?

Neither is better overall. Mirror is bright and easy to inspect; satin hides wear.

Can finishes include a logo?

Logos can usually be laser marked or etched, which lasts better than printing.
