Surgical probes are slender instruments used to explore wounds, sinus tracts, fistulas and cavities. Grooved directors are related instruments with a channel along their length that guides a scalpel or scissors safely. Both are inexpensive, but having the right sizes in a set saves time and improves safety.

In short

Probes explore; directors guide. Malleable probes bend to follow a tract, ball-tipped ends reduce the risk of creating a false passage, and grooved directors protect underlying tissue when cutting.

Types of surgical probes

  • Malleable probes: soft, bendable probes, often double-ended with a ball tip and an eye, that can be shaped to follow a tract.
  • Probe-pointed and ball-tipped probes: rigid or semi-rigid probes with rounded ends for exploring wounds without piercing tissue.
  • Fistula probes: slim probes, sometimes with an eye for passing a seton or suture, used in anal fistula surgery.
  • Lacrimal probes: very fine probes used in eye and tear duct procedures.

Grooved directors

A grooved director has a channel along its length. It is passed under tissue, for example along a fistula tract or under a fascial layer, and the surgeon cuts along the groove with a scalpel or scissors. The director protects the tissue beneath. Some patterns include a tongue-tie (frenulum) slot at the handle end.

Materials

Malleable probes are made from a softer metal or annealed stainless steel so they bend without breaking. Rigid probes and directors are made from stainless steel. Silver and silver-alloy probes are also traditional because they bend easily.

Before you buy surgical probes

  • The tips are smooth and rounded, with no sharp edges.
  • Malleable probes bend smoothly and return to shape when straightened.
  • Eyes are smooth inside, so sutures and setons do not fray.
  • The groove on directors is even along its length.

Frequently asked questions

How long are surgical probes?

Common lengths range from about 13 to 20 cm. Lacrimal probes are much finer and shorter.

Can malleable probes be straightened after use?

Yes, they can be straightened by hand, but repeated bending at the same point eventually weakens them.

Are probes included in minor surgery sets?

Many incision and drainage and minor surgery sets include a malleable probe for exploring abscess cavities.
