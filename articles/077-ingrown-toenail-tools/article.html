Ingrown toenails are a common and painful problem. Podiatrists and trained pedicurists use a small group of fine instruments to clear and trim the edge of the nail. Ingrown toenail tools are designed to reach into the narrow groove beside the nail without injuring the skin.

Important

Infected, very painful or recurring ingrown toenails, and any ingrown nail in people with diabetes or poor circulation, should be treated by a podiatrist or doctor.

Ingrown nail nippers

These nippers have narrow, pointed jaws that can slide under the edge of the nail. They are made with straight or slightly curved jaws and with fine or extra-fine points. Sharp, accurately aligned tips are essential, because they must cut a small sliver of nail without catching the skin.

Nail lifters and files

A nail lifter has a thin, smooth, rounded end that slips under the nail edge to lift it or guide a file or nipper. Ingrown nail files are narrow so they can smooth the edge of the nail inside the groove.

Quality checklist

  • Nipper tips are fine, sharp and meet exactly.
  • Lifters and probes have smooth, rounded ends.
  • Files are narrow, with an even cut.
  • All tools are stainless steel and can be sterilized between clients.

Frequently asked questions

Can I treat my own ingrown toenail with these tools?

Mild cases are sometimes managed at home, but infected, painful or recurring ingrown nails should be seen by a podiatrist or doctor.

What is the difference between ingrown nail nippers and ordinary nippers?

Ingrown nail nippers have narrow, pointed jaws that reach the nail edge in the groove; ordinary nippers are wider.

How should professionals reprocess these tools?

Clean and sterilize them between clients according to local regulations.
