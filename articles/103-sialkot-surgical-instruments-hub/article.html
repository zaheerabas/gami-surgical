Sialkot, a city in the Punjab province of Pakistan, is one of the world's best-known centres for making surgical, dental and beauty instruments. Instruments made in Sialkot are used in hospitals, clinics and salons around the world, often under the brands of companies in other countries. For buyers, Sialkot surgical instruments offer a wide choice of manufacturers and products.

In short

Sialkot has a long tradition of metalworking skills, a dense network of specialised workshops and suppliers, and decades of experience exporting worldwide. Quality varies between manufacturers, so buyers should choose carefully.

A long tradition

Instrument making in Sialkot dates back more than a century, growing from local metalworking and repair skills into a large export industry. Skills such as forging, fitting and finishing have been passed down through families and workshops, building deep expertise in hand finishing.

A complete supply chain in one city

Sialkot's strength lies in its cluster of businesses: forging units, machining workshops, heat treatment, polishing, plating and coating, packaging, and exporters, all close together. This makes it possible to produce a very wide range of instruments, from standard patterns to custom designs, efficiently.

Quality varies: choose carefully

Because there are many manufacturers, quality ranges from basic to excellent. The best results come from working with a manufacturer that controls its processes, uses the right steel, heat treats and passivates properly, and inspects every instrument.

Choosing a Sialkot manufacturer

  • Ask about steel grades, heat treatment and passivation.
  • Request samples and test them.
  • Ask about quality management and certifications that matter in your market.
  • Check communication, lead times and shipping experience.
