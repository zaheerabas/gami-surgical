Nail scissors and nail clippers both trim nails, but they cut in different ways. Nail scissors give control and follow the curve of the nail, while clippers are quick and easy to use. Many people, salons and retailers keep both. This guide explains the differences.

In short

Curved nail scissors are ideal for shaping fingernails precisely. Clippers are fast and easy, especially for toenails. Thick toenails need heavy-duty clippers or nippers.

Nail scissors

Nail scissors have short, strong blades, usually curved to follow the shape of the nail. They cut in small snips, which gives control over the shape. Straight nail scissors are used for cutting toenails straight across. Fine cuticle scissors are a different tool, with very thin blades for hangnails and cuticles.

Nail clippers

Lever clippers have two curved cutting edges that close when the lever is pressed. They are quick to use and need little skill. Toenail clippers are larger, with a wider jaw opening and often straight jaws.

Quality points

Before you buy

  • The blades or jaws meet evenly along the whole edge.
  • The cut is clean, not crushing or splitting the nail.
  • Scissors open and close smoothly with a firm screw.
  • Stainless steel that can be cleaned and disinfected.

Professional use

In salons, tools used on clients must be cleaned and disinfected between uses, following local rules. Stainless steel nail scissors and clippers that tolerate disinfection are essential.

Frequently asked questions

Are nail scissors better than clippers?

Neither is better for everything. Scissors give more control; clippers are quicker. Many people use scissors for fingernails and clippers for toenails.

Why are toenails cut straight across?

Cutting straight across, without rounding the corners too deeply, is commonly advised to reduce the risk of ingrown toenails.

Can nail scissors be sharpened?

Good quality steel nail scissors can be sharpened by a professional.
