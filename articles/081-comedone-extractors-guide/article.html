Comedone extractors are small stainless steel tools used by estheticians and skin care professionals to remove blackheads and whiteheads during facials. A well-made extractor applies even pressure around the comedone, which is gentler on the skin than squeezing with fingers.

In short

Comedone extractors have a loop or spoon end to press out comedones, and sometimes a fine lancet. Smooth finishing, the right loop size and strict hygiene between clients are essential.

Common designs

EndUse
Round loopPressing around blackheads with even pressure
Oval or angled loopReaching the nose and contours
Spoon with hole (Schamberg / Unna type)Flat pressure around the comedone
Fine lancetUsed by trained professionals to open closed comedones

Most comedone extractors are double-ended, combining two loops or a loop and a lancet.

Using comedone extractors professionally

Skin is usually prepared and softened first. The loop is placed around the comedone and pressed gently and evenly. Excessive pressure can bruise or mark the skin. Lancets should only be used by trained professionals, and the rules about which treatments estheticians may perform vary by country and region.

Hygiene

Extractors come into contact with skin and sometimes with fluids, so they must be cleaned and disinfected or sterilized between clients according to local rules. Single-use lancets are often preferred where a lancet is needed.

Quality checklist

  • Loops are smooth and rounded, with no sharp edges.
  • The loop size suits the area being treated.
  • The handle is textured for a secure grip.
  • Stainless steel that tolerates cleaning and sterilization.

Frequently asked questions

Are comedone extractors safe?

Used gently by trained professionals with proper hygiene, they are a standard facial tool. Aggressive use can mark the skin.

What size loop is best?

Smaller loops suit small comedones and the nose; larger loops suit larger areas. Double-ended tools offer two sizes.

Can extractors be sterilized?

Stainless steel comedone extractors can be cleaned and steam sterilized.
