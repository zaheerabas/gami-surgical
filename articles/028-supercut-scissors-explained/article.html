Supercut scissors have one blade ground to a razor-sharp edge, while the other blade usually has a fine micro-serration or standard edge. The combination gives a very smooth, precise cut with little effort and minimal trauma to tissue. Many manufacturers mark supercut scissors with black handles so staff can recognise them at a glance.

In short

Supercut scissors cut delicate tissue very cleanly. They are ideal for plastic, vascular and fine general surgery, but the razor edge needs careful handling and specialist sharpening.

How supercut scissors work

A standard surgical scissor blade has a bevelled edge that cuts by shearing against the other blade. In supercut scissors, one blade is ground thinner and sharper, like a razor. As the blades close, this edge slices cleanly through tissue rather than pinching it. The opposing blade often has fine serrations that hold the tissue so it does not slide forward.

Where supercut scissors are worth it

  • Plastic and reconstructive surgery, where clean cuts help healing.
  • Vascular and fine general surgery.
  • Delicate dissecting scissors such as Metzenbaum and iris patterns.

They are not the right choice for sutures, dressings or heavy tissue, which will damage the razor edge.

Caring for supercut scissors

Care checklist

  • Keep them for tissue only and label them clearly in sets.
  • Use tip guards and avoid contact with other instruments in trays.
  • Clean them open with a soft brush; never use abrasive pads.
  • Send them only to repair services that can restore a supercut edge.

Frequently asked questions

Are black handles always supercut?

Black handles are commonly used for supercut scissors, but some manufacturers use black coatings for other reasons, such as reducing glare. Check the product description.

Can supercut scissors be sharpened?

Yes, but they need a specialist who can regrind the razor edge. General sharpening can turn them into ordinary scissors.

Which supercut scissors should I start with?

Supercut Metzenbaum and iris scissors are the most widely used.
