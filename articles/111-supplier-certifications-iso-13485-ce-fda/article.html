When buying surgical or dental instruments, especially for regulated markets, buyers often ask about supplier certifications. Terms like ISO 13485, CE marking and FDA registration are widely used but often misunderstood. This guide explains what each one means and how to check it.

In short

ISO 13485 certifies a quality management system. CE marking shows that a product meets EU requirements. FDA registration and listing relate to selling in the United States. Always verify certificates and check what products and scope they cover.

The main certifications

CertificationWhat it meansWhat it does not mean
ISO 13485The company's quality management system for medical devices has been audited by a certification bodyIt is not a product approval
CE markingThe product meets EU requirements (under the Medical Device Regulation for medical devices)It does not cover products outside the declared scope
FDA registration and listingThe establishment is registered with the US FDA and products are listedRegistration alone is not FDA "approval"
ISO 9001A general quality management systemIt is not specific to medical devices

Verifying supplier certifications

Verification checklist

  • Ask for copies of certificates and check the company name and address.
  • Check the scope: which products and activities are covered.
  • Check expiry dates and the issuing certification body.
  • Verify certificates with the issuing body or public databases where available.
  • For CE-marked devices, ask for the Declaration of Conformity.

Which certifications do you need?

It depends on your market and products. Beauty tools usually do not need medical device certifications, while surgical and dental instruments sold as medical devices usually do. Importers and distributors may also have obligations of their own. Ask your regulator or a regulatory consultant what applies.

Frequently asked questions

Is an ISO 13485 certificate the same as CE marking?

No. ISO 13485 covers the quality system; CE marking covers product conformity in the EU. They often go together but are different.

Does FDA registration mean a product is FDA approved?

No. Many instruments are registered and listed without needing premarket approval. Registration is not approval.

Can a certificate be checked online?

Many certification bodies and regulators offer online databases. Use them when available.
